# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Customers looking for personalized hair brushes, claw clips, and gift-ready styling sets. They need to browse products quickly, understand the available customization path, and submit a custom-order request with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Norie presents handmade-to-order hair accessories and guides visitors from product discovery to a clear customization request. Success means the product imagery feels desirable, the catalog is easy to scan, and every order action leads naturally to the Customize page.

## Brand Personality

Playful, polished, and intimate. The experience should feel warm and giftable without becoming childish, ornate, or impersonal.

## Anti-references

- Dense marketplace listings with competing badges, descriptions, and controls.
- Generic luxury-editorial layouts that make products harder to compare.
- Rounded app-style cards, oversized controls, or decorative interface effects that distract from the accessories.
- Low-contrast minimalism that sacrifices readability for softness.

## Design Principles

1. Let product imagery lead and keep supporting information concise.
2. Make customization the obvious next step from every product.
3. Use consistent catalog structure so sets and individual pieces are easy to compare.
4. Preserve a handmade, personal tone while keeping interactions precise.
5. Treat accessibility and readable contrast as part of the brand's care for customers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Maintain keyboard-operable carousels and actions, visible focus indicators, informative image alternatives, adequate color contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive reflow at 320 CSS pixels.
