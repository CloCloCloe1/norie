# Norie Toronto Helicopter Video Prompt Design

## Purpose

Create a platform-neutral AI video prompt inspired by the supplied reference video while replacing its brand content and location.

## Format

- Vertical 9:16 video
- Approximately 9 seconds
- One continuous shot
- Bright daytime commercial aesthetic

## Scene

A recognizable Toronto skyline under a clear blue summer sky, with the CN Tower clearly visible. A glossy pink helicopter carries an oversized pale-pink luxury shopping bag suspended by sturdy straps.

## Motion and Composition

The helicopter and bag begin far from the camera and fly steadily closer in one continuous shot. The bag sways subtly in the rotor wash while remaining upright. The subject grows gradually from a small distant element to the dominant centered foreground object. The camera remains mostly stable with slight natural aerial-advertising realism.

## Brand Treatment

The shopping bag uses a refined pink palette with bold black typography. Intended copy:

1. `Norie`
2. `Your Name in Sparkle`
3. `Custom rhinestone combs & claw clips`
4. `Made to order`

Because generative video models often distort long text, deliver two prompt variants:

- A direct-text version requesting the exact copy.
- A clean-bag version with reserved typography space for accurate post-production text.

## Visual Character

Playful, feminine, polished, luxurious, and slightly surreal. Photorealistic lighting and environment, believable helicopter motion, glossy pink surfaces, subtle sparkle highlights, crisp commercial color grading, and no scene cuts.

## Exclusions

No warped helicopter, broken rotor blades, extra straps, duplicated bags, distorted skyline, incorrect landmark, illegible random text, aggressive camera shake, night lighting, fog, rain, or scene transitions.
